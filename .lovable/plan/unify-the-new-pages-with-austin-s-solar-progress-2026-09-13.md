# Unify the new pages with Austin's Solar Progress

## Goal
Make the recent research pages and course feel like the established Austin Clean Energy site, using Austin's Solar Progress as the visual reference.

## Shared styling
- Create one shared page stylesheet for typography, content width, title spacing, section rhythm, and expandable rows.
- Use the site's sans-serif typography for all page and section headings.
- Match the reference page's `max-w-5xl` content width and responsive horizontal spacing.
- Keep boxes only for interactive controls, charts that need containment, and repeated selectable items.
- Standardize accessible expandable rows with the same spacing, borders, focus state, and plus/minus indicator.

## Page updates
- Apply the shared styling to Pricing Pressure, Load Growth Estimator, Case for Austin Energy, Peaker vs. Battery, and The Grid Primer.
- Remove project-specific labels such as “Secret Vote · Explainer” and replace them with concise Austin Clean Energy labels or no label.
- Tighten introductory and supporting copy without changing calculations, controls, charts, sources, or course behavior.
- Replace em dashes in visible copy on these pages with clearer punctuation or shorter sentences.
- Keep methodology, caveats, source notes, and nonessential callouts in expandable sections.

## Validation
- Check all five pages on desktop and mobile.
- Verify disclosures, sliders, year controls, course navigation, charts, and tooltips.
- Confirm consistent widths, no overflow, and no browser console errors.
