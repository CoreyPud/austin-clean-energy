# Streamline the four new research pages

## Goal
Bring Pricing Pressure, Load Growth Estimator, Case for Austin Energy, and Peaker vs. Battery into the established Austin Clean Energy visual system while preserving every calculation, chart, control, and source.

## Design direction
- Use the existing Newsreader, IBM Plex Sans, and IBM Plex Mono typography.
- Keep the Austin green semantic palette, with orange reserved for warnings and gas/peaker comparisons.
- Recompose each page as open chapter bands separated by spacing, rules, or subtle background shifts.
- Replace boxed metric cards with open, ruled metric strips.
- Remove decorative containers and shadows around charts; retain containment only where an interactive control genuinely needs it.
- Keep all core findings, charts, tables, and controls immediately visible.

## Expandable supporting information
- Convert caveats, interpretation notes, methodology, limitations, and source lists into accessible expandable rows.
- Use concise summaries so readers can decide whether to open supporting detail.
- Keep important warning labels visible while placing their longer explanation inside the expandable area.
- Preserve keyboard access, focus states, and reduced-motion behavior.

## Page-specific changes
- **Pricing Pressure:** streamline historical metrics and charts; turn the scenario interpretation, methodology, and source details into expandable sections.
- **Load Growth Estimator:** keep scenario inputs visible; move the long baseline caveat, methodology, and sources into expandable sections; simplify result framing.
- **Case for Austin Energy:** make the legal-status notice expandable, remove boxes from charts/figures/quotes, and collapse long chart notes and sources.
- **Peaker vs. Battery:** remove the repeated panel-card treatment, use chapter dividers, and collapse long interpretive notes plus the final methodology/source material.

## Validation
- Check all four pages at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Verify expand/collapse controls, year tabs, sliders, charts, and tooltips still work.
- Confirm no horizontal overflow, overlapping text, or browser console errors.
